This pack was generated on 2026-10-28 by the Real Tracks track-pack builder from open data. In-game it is called "Austin".

## USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP)
Terrain heights and track heights come from the USGS 3DEP 1 m lidar-derived DEM (National Map ImageServer).
Public domain: work of the United States Government (USGS). https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program

## USGS NAIP imagery
The orthophoto (ortho.jpg) and the loading screen are derived from the USGS National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP) mosaic served by the
National Map (USDA Farm Service Agency NAIP imagery). Public domain: work of the United States Government.
https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/science/usgs-eros-archive-aerial-photography-national-agriculture-imagery-program-naip

## Copernicus DEM
Not used in this pack (the US lidar DEM above was available). Packs outside the US may use the Copernicus GLO-30 DEM (c) DLR e.V. / ESA and
contain the Copernicus attribution here.

## Game numbers
The Locations-row values (tyres, wear, safety car odds, stat weights) are derived by the builder with a stated reason for each, in manifest.json.
